How the editor works

You can change the words and photos on the website from here, without any code.

Changing words

  • Open Pages and choose a page on the left.
  • Move your mouse over the page. Anything with a dashed blue outline can be changed. Click it and type.
  • The black toolbar can make words bold or italic, add a link, change size, alignment or color.
  • Press Done (or the Esc key) when you're finished with a piece of text.
  • The curved arrow puts that one piece of text back the way it is on the live site.

Lists

For bullet points, the states list, office boxes and the facts on the home page, the toolbar also shows Add one (copies the item you're in) and Remove.

Photos

Click a photo, then Choose a new photo. It's shrunk to a web-friendly size automatically. Please add a short description of the photo for visitors who use screen readers.

Phone, address and team

These show up in many places, so they have their own tabs: Business details and Team. Clicking them on a page takes you there.

Publishing

Your changes are saved on this computer as you go. When you're happy, open Publish, enter the editor password and press Publish now. The live website updates in about a minute.

Preview site (top right) shows the whole website with your unpublished changes, so you can click around before publishing.
