Careers

Choose your career path

Affiliated agents

As an Affiliated Agent, you become part of the Good Harbor team, with access to valuable resources and support to help grow your business. This includes individualized training, comprehensive marketing tools, and expert case design and management, so you can provide the best solutions for your clients.

You'll also be able to use Good Harbor's established branding to strengthen your credibility and market presence, and you'll receive competitive compensation. It's an ideal choice if you want to be part of a collaborative, well-supported team.

Independent agents

As an Independent Agent, you keep full control of your brand and business while benefiting from essential back-office support. Continue operating under your existing branding while receiving administrative and operational help that streamlines your workflow.

Independent agents also enjoy competitive compensation and the flexibility to work autonomously. This structure is perfect if you prefer independence but still want support that helps you succeed.
