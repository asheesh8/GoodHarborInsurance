Coverage built for the cannabis industry

Whether you're a cultivator, manufacturer, or retailer, the right cannabis insurance protects your business from financial loss, regulatory challenges, and unexpected disruptions. Our experts will help you navigate the complexities and build a coverage plan that meets your needs.

General liability, product liability and recall

General liability insurance protects against third-party claims for bodily injury, property damage, and legal expenses. Businesses that manufacture or sell cannabis products also need product liability insurance to cover claims of defective or harmful products, including health concerns or mislabeled goods. Product recall insurance covers the cost of recalling defective or contaminated products, preventing significant financial loss and reputational damage.

Crime and property insurance

Cannabis businesses often handle large cash transactions, making them a target for theft and fraud. Crime and fidelity insurance protects against losses from employee theft, robbery, or fraud. Property insurance covers grow facilities, dispensaries, equipment, and inventory against fire, vandalism, and other damage.

Workers' compensation and crop loss

Workers' compensation covers medical expenses and lost wages if an employee is injured on the job, and helps you stay compliant with state rules. Because cannabis crops aren't covered under standard agricultural policies, specialized crop loss insurance protects cultivators against fire, theft, equipment failure, and natural disasters.
